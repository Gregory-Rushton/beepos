Welcome to Bee Positive Apiary

We're a small, family operated apiary keeping bees in Easton MA. We started in 2019 with two colonies at our home and have since grown to include two other locations, one in Easton and another in Norton MA.

We strive to keep our bees healthy in an ethical and sustainable manner. We work closely with local beekeeping clubs and other beekeepers to help educate the public about the benefits of bees and other pollinators.
