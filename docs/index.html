Bee Positive Apiary

Welcome to Bee Positive Apiary

We're a small, family operated apiary keeping bees in Easton and Norton, MA. We started in 2019 with two colonies at our home in Easton and have since added a second location in Norton. We currently run about 10 to 12 colonies each year, evenly split between the two locations.

We strive to keep our bees healthy in an ethical and sustainable manner. We work closely with local beekeeping clubs and other beekeepers to help educate the public about the benefits of bees and other pollinators.
