Thinking about keeping bees?

The best first step is joining a local beekeeping club. Clubs run beginner courses, often in late winter so new beekeepers are ready when bees arrive in spring, and they connect you with experienced members who can answer questions. The Massachusetts Beekeepers Association keeps a list of county clubs.

How to help pollinators at home

  • Plant flowers that bloom at different times from spring through fall, choosing native plants where you can.
  • Let clover and dandelions bloom in your lawn, and mow a little less often.
  • Avoid spraying pesticides on plants in bloom. If you must spray, do it in the evening, when bees aren't foraging.
  • Put out a shallow dish of water with pebbles or stones so bees have somewhere to land.
  • Leave some bare ground and old plant stems over winter. Many native bees nest in the soil or in hollow stems.
